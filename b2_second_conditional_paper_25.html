Before you begin

Team information

Enter each participating student. Student 1 is required. For any additional member, complete both fields. Your team submits one shared set of answers.

Responses are saved in this browser tab where storage is available. Avoid private browsing and keep this tab open.

Your assessment guide

  1. Complete five questions on each page. You may revisit earlier pages.
  2. Choose answers or write a complete sentence as instructed. Either clause order is accepted in written answers.
  3. No answers or scores appear until you submit. There is no countdown.
  4. Keep this page in focus. Avoid copying, pasting, printing, or changing apps during the attempt.
Checking and device information

Written answers use a limited rule-based checker. Unrecognized responses receive teacher review. Use the supplied ideas and subjects; do not rely on unrestricted paraphrasing.

Fullscreen is requested when available. iOS and some browsers restrict fullscreen and interruption detection. This page cannot detect every device or browser action.

Duration includes interruptions. Question times estimate active interaction, exclude passive reading, and cap inactivity at 30 seconds.

Names, answers, timings and integrity events are recorded. If Google Sheets is configured, the report is sent to the teacher's endpoint.
